Geography Grind

There is a virus in New York City that is making people turn into zombies! You are trying to escape by taking a plane, but everyone is also trying to get as far away from the city as possible! Airlines have limited seats and are overwhelmed, so they create a way to decide how far people get to travel. The cumulative number of points you get in the next four geography games represents the number of miles you traveled. Keep track of the number of points you acquire in each specific game - it will calculate the sum at the end. There will be instructions throughout the games.

Instructions for Game 1

In the next game, there will be a scrambled country name, and you must figure it out by typing in your answer. Get as many as possible in the alotted 30 seconds. The timer will begin as soon as you press the button below!
